# PROJECT A.R.E.S. — Prototype Scope and Research Context

- **Project:** Astronaut Resistance & Exercise System.
- **Event context:** NASA Space Apps prototype; November 14–15 team sprint.
- **Project focus:** Exercise coaching and workout logging for delayed-support environments. This description is not proof of selection for a named official challenge or of judging eligibility.
- **Team:** Two development members, two frontend members and two documentation members.

## What the prototype demonstrates

A.R.E.S. combines client-side Google MediaPipe pose estimates, deterministic joint-angle rules and rep counting with local workout history and game expedition progress. A three-step profile wizard generates rule-based routine suggestions for review. This is a demonstration, not operational astronaut medical software or a clinically validated injury-prevention system.

The app does not measure muscle mass or bone density. Its Earth-Baseline Strength Retention score is a team-defined heuristic combining logged load/Earth-1RM ratios and recorded form scores. The 75%, 82% and 88% scenario goals, 15% limitation moderation, suggested loads and joint-angle thresholds are prototype choices. They are not NASA standards, medical clearance, or validated injury treatment. Vitals and BMI are recorded context, not diagnostic inputs to a clinical model.

## Research context and limits

- NASA describes 2.5 hours of scheduled daily ISS exercise supporting crew health. This motivates the problem; it is not the duration or a medical prescription generated by this app. See [NASA astronaut health care](https://www.nasa.gov/missions/station/faq-the-real-story-about-astronaut-health-care-in-space/).
- NASA distinguishes signal delays from periods of unavailable communications. This project's manually selected 24-minute blackout is a resilience scenario, without a countdown, physical latency simulation or NASA Mission Control connection. See [NASA communications analysis](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20220013418).
- The virtual routes use approximate Valles Marineris length (4,000 km) and Olympus Mons diameter (600 km). The 250 qualified kg-to-1 km conversion is a game mechanic, and the schematic artwork is not terrain-derived. See [NASA/JPL Mars dimensions](https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/press_kits/insight/landing/facts/mars-at-a-glance/).
- Motivation and reduced manual logging are design hypotheses. No astronaut, clinical, adherence or injury-outcome study of this prototype has been performed.

## Delivered modules

| Module | Demonstrated behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Command Center | Sample schedule, local completion, routine rationale, workout-derived score and scenario comparison |
| AI Workout Arena | Live or simulated 33-point pose estimates, configurable load, reps, timer, rule-based feedback and optional local voice |
| Expedition Journey | Game distance, schematic route artwork and locked/unlocked milestones |
| Medical Telemetry | Rolling workout-derived scores, volume/symmetry summaries and JSON/CSV/print exports carrying prototype context |
| Workout Logs & History | Local history, filters, pagination, session details and estimated-angle snapshot replay |
| Profile & Mission Settings | Editable profile/context, reviewed routine recalibration, local data controls and optional summary sync |

Frame rate depends on hardware, browser and model inference time; a device benchmark is needed before making a live FPS claim. No webcam video is recorded. Local pose snapshots are retained for replay, while optional Supabase sync sends summary fields. The initial 30-day history is synthetic demo data.

An already loaded app can keep operating without network connectivity. No service worker is implemented, so uncached starts and offline reloads are not guaranteed. Installed local voices and browser camera/WASM support determine which optional features are available.

## Potential future applications

Remote exercise logging and home fitness are possible future directions requiring user research and validation. Clinical rehabilitation, diagnosis, safe-load prescription and operational astronaut use are not established capabilities. Official sources provide context; they do not endorse the app or validate its algorithms.
