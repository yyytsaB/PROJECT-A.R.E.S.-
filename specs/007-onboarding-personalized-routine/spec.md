# M6 — Pre-Space Onboarding & Personalized Routine

- **Status:** APPROVED — detailed user implementation request, November 14, 2026.
- **Dependencies:** Completed M0–M5 and existing six public seams.

## Intent and boundaries
Add a three-step review-and-confirm onboarding flow, deterministic rule-based prototype routine suggestions, dashboard explanations, and manual/automatic recalibration. Reuse pure telemetry, catalog, local mission persistence and headless hooks. Preserve the existing 56 tests without changing their assertions or fixtures. No clinical diagnosis, NASA Flight Surgeon clearance, validated injury treatment, remote services, raw video, decorative styling or new dependencies. Vitals/BMI are recorded context, not diagnostic triggers. Routine targets are suggestions; existing manually configured arena load and locked retention math remain unchanged.

## Contracts and defaults
- **Seam 4:** `calculateBmi(bodyWeightKg: number, heightCm: number): number`, one decimal, positive finite inputs. `generatePersonalizedRoutine(profile: AstronautProfile, sessions: readonly WorkoutSessionRecord[], nowIso: string): PersonalizedRoutinePlan` is pure and deterministic, rejects invalid profile/time inputs, and considers only the existing 30-day reporting window, excluding future records.
- `PersonalizedRoutinePlan`: `routineId`, `generatedAtIso`, exact `summaryLabel: 'Personalized for you — Adapted to your progress'`, prototype disclaimer and `items: readonly PersonalizedRoutineItem[]`. Items contain exerciseId/name/kind/scheduledTime, targetSets/targetReps/recommendedLoadKg/targetCardioKm and rationale.
- M6 profile fields are optional in the legacy TypeScript input contract, normalized on load/create/seed: heightCm 175, restingHeartRateBpm 60, systolicBpMmHg 118, diastolicBpMmHg 76, injuryLimitations NONE, injuryNotes empty, missionType MARS_TRANSIT_360D, missionDurationDays 360, autoAdjustRoutine true. Mission-state routineNotification defaults empty; personalizedRoutine is optional for legacy core states. Missing fields default; explicit invalid fields are rejected.
- **Seam 5:** persist a reviewed plan and settings locally. Store defaults implicitly (omit default-valued M6 fields from serialized JSON) and use that same serialization for measured cache bytes, preserving old exact byte fixtures. Hydrate defaults on load. Validate optional stored routines, enum values, positive finite metrics, integer positive mission duration, and systolic > diastolic. Validate context values without diagnosing physiological ranges.

## Prototype rules (team-defined, not validated medical prescriptions)
Static content identifies exercise focus, starting cardio distances and affected exercises for each limitation. Resistance suggestions start at 80% of the entered Earth baseline with 4 sets × 12 reps when no recent per-exercise history exists. With history, use the latest configured resistance load; suggest 5% progression when its per-exercise prototype retention reaches the selected scenario goal, otherwise hold that load. Cap suggestions at 90% of entered baseline. Applicable limitation rules reduce the resulting load or cardio distance by 15%, explicitly stating the moderation and prototype nature. Knee mapping: SQUAT and TREADMILL; lower-back mapping: SQUAT, DEADLIFT and ROWING; shoulder mapping: OVERHEAD_PRESS, BICEP_CURL and ROWING. Cardio starts at 3 km treadmill, 5 km cycle, 2 km rowing, or the latest positive per-modality distance. All targets round to one decimal. Gravity selects the existing prototype comparison goal and rationale; never multiply ARED resistance by ambient g. Mission type/duration and recorded metrics inform context explanations rather than unsupported clinical rules.

## Browser/headless acceptance (Seam 6)
1. Accessible “Mission Onboarding — Set Up Your Profile” section: Step 1 of 3 contains name, height/weight, live labeled BMI, resting heart rate and systolic/diastolic pressure; Step 2 contains all Earth baselines, limitation enum and notes; Step 3 contains mission type/duration and gravity. Next/Previous validate the current step and preserve draft values. No draft profile is persisted before confirmation.
2. Generate My Routine shows “A.R.E.S. is building your personalized program…” in a polite live region for a configurable delay (default 150ms), then shows targets and rationale. Confirm & Apply Routine validates and saves both the reviewed profile and plan, announcing success. Editing/backing out invalidates a prior preview; unmount/clear/seed or a newer generation cannot apply a stale delayed result.
3. Initialize a stored dashboard plan when none exists. Under Today’s schedule show the exact summary label, per-exercise prescriptions/rationales, and a clear rule-based prototype disclaimer. Keep schedule checkboxes, launch behavior and manual arena load compatible.
4. Saving a completed workout recalculates the stored routine and sets “Your routine has been updated based on your recent progress.” when autoAdjustRoutine is true; when false, persist the workout but keep the stored plan unchanged until Recalibrate Routine. Recalibration uses latest profile/history and always announces the same status. Background sync must preserve concurrent plan/profile updates.
5. Settings adds an Update Health Metrics section with editable height/BMI/vitals, limitations/notes, mission type/duration, associated field errors, Recalibrate Routine, and persisted Auto-adjust routine based on progress checkbox. Invalid edits cannot enter the store or trigger recalibration using stale valid values; changing metadata does not silently recalculate a paused/manual plan.
